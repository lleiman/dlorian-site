Many arms, many heads. Old ways of drawing power, rebuilt by a machine that has never knelt in a temple. It gets the gods wrong, and the wrongness is the interesting part.

Shiva, totem, iconostasis — all of them ask the same question: how much more of a man should there be than there is.

It does not know the canon, so it is not afraid to break it.
